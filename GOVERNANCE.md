# Governance

Attow Network is initially stewarded by its repository owner. Named maintainers, a separate governing entity and voting procedures have not been established. This document does not invent those roles or imply a foundation or commercial operator exists.

Routine changes are reviewed for correctness, tests, attribution and compatibility with the documented architecture. Changes to canonical encodings, effect/authority semantics, licensing, public protocols and release support policy require an ADR that explains alternatives and migration consequences. Maintainers should record the decision and evidence in the repository.

Authority safety and honest implementation status take precedence over demonstration claims. No change may make replay dispatch real effects, duplicate rights through forks or treat ambiguous provider outcomes as known failures merely to improve availability. The [working rules](AGENTS.md) and [architecture](docs/architecture.md) state the current invariants.

Contributors retain attribution for their work under the project licenses. The repository owner controls release authorization and access until an explicit successor governance policy is adopted. Changes to this policy should be documented with their rationale and take effect prospectively.
