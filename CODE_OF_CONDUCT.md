# Code of conduct

Participants in Attow Network collaboration are expected to treat one another with respect, discuss technical disagreements in good faith, credit others' work and make participation accessible to people with different backgrounds and experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publication of private information without permission are not acceptable. Critique behavior and technical decisions rather than attacking individuals. Respect requests to stop unwanted contact.

The repository owner or designated maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or revoke access in response to violations. Enforcement should consider context, be proportionate, protect reporters' privacy, and avoid retaliatory action. A maintainer involved in a dispute should step aside from reviewing it when another responsible maintainer is available.

Report concerns through a verified maintainer-configured private contact route. At the 0.1.0 candidate checkpoint, no private moderation route or named moderation team has been verified. Ask the repository owner to establish a route without publishing sensitive details. The owner-authorized repository destination is `sharb1235-hash/attow-network`; creating it does not itself configure private conduct reporting. Establishing and testing a usable route remains a launch condition in the [checklist](docs/launch-checklist.md).

This policy covers project collaboration spaces and representation of the project at related events. It is an original project policy, not a claim of certification or affiliation with another conduct framework.
